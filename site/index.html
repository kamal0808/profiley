How it works

Three steps. Then you forget it's there.

01

Install

It finds the browser you use and your profiles in it, pictures and all. Nothing to set up.

02

Click any link

A new site asks which profile. Click one, or press 1, 2 or 3.

03

Tick to remember

Next time, that site opens straight in the right profile. No asking.

Change your mind

Hold Shift while clicking a link to get the popup back for a saved site.

See every rule

Open Profiley from Spotlight to edit, remove or add saved sites by hand.

No extension

It sits in place of your default browser. Nothing to install inside Chrome.

Stays on your Mac

Your saved sites live in one file on your Mac. No account, no tracking.

Free, open source, set up in two minutes

Profiley is made by one person and every line of its code is on GitHub for anyone to read. The only thing it sends is an anonymous daily count, and you can turn that off.

It isn’t signed by Apple yet (that costs ₹8,700 a year), so your Mac asks you to confirm once. When people start paying for Profiley, the signature is the first thing it buys.

Here’s the whole setup

Your Mac asks to make Profiley your default browser: say yes. That’s how links reach it, and it hands every one straight to your own browser, in the right profile.

Ready?

Download, drag it into Applications, open it. The steps stay on the next page if you need them.
